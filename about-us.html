Welcome to the White Water Rogue State. We're a group of former Liverpool University Canoe Club members who run frequent peer paddles in North Wales and the Lake District. We very much encourage you to sign up to the official LUCC trips when they happen, but from our experience people progress much faster and enjoy white water a lot more if they also get involved with peer paddles. And since these smaller paddles are much easier to organize they can happen almost weekly.
